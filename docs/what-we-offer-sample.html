A few things you might be wondering

Can we start with a small first version?

Yes. We identify the functionality that matters most and agree on a practical first scope. Feedback from real use helps guide the next improvements.

Can you improve a system we already use?

We can review your existing website or application, understand what gets in the way, and recommend improvements based on your business needs.

How do we describe what we want?

Start with the problem you want to solve. You can also sketch a layout in the website builder and send your design through its sharing controls. We review it with you before agreeing on scope and an estimate.
